Idea Map: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Idea Map is a free flowchart and idea-mapping web app. This page explains what happens to your data. In short: your maps stay on your device, or in your own Google Drive if you choose that. We don't run a server that receives your maps.

Where your maps are stored

  • In your browser. The app automatically saves your current map in your browser's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device unless you choose to save it elsewhere. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.
  • As files you save. "Save" and "Load" use ordinary files on your device.
  • In your Google Drive (optional). If you use "Save to Google Drive" or "Open from Google Drive", you sign in with your Google account. Maps are stored as .json files in an "Idea Maps" folder in your own Drive.

What the Google Drive feature accesses

The app requests only the drive.file permission. This lets the app see and change only the files and folders that it created itself. It cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files. We don't receive your Google password. We don't see your name or email address, and we don't store them.

The sign-in token stays in your browser's memory for the current session. It is used only to talk to Google Drive from your browser. We don't collect it, store it on a server or share it.

What we do not do

  • We don't collect, sell or share your maps or personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't use your Google data for advertising or to train AI models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Hosting

The app is served from a web host. Like any website, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operations. Those logs don't include the contents of your maps.

Deleting your data

  • Browser copy: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Google Drive copy: delete the files, or the "Idea Maps" folder, in Google Drive.
  • Access: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Yehuda-Zev/idea-map/issues.
